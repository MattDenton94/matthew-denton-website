Our story

Somewhere that feels like home, and like a faraway jungle.

Selva was created to bring friends, family and strangers together. Every dish, from our Selva breakfast to our bennies, burgers and salads, is made with the finest ingredients and "a little bit of this and a little bit of that".

Pull up a chair under the hanging plants, order a latte, and stay a while.

🌿 100% halal, every single dish
