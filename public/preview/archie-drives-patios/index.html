Meet Archie

The man who quotes is on site building it.

Eduart, known to everyone as Archie, served his apprenticeship in Greece and earned the Diploma of Mastercraftsman. He spent 25 years running his own business there, building villas, stone archways and crazy paving steps, before bringing that craft to Manchester.

Today he and his small team work across Denton, Tameside and Stockport. Archie visits, quotes and works on site with the team, so the standard never drops.
