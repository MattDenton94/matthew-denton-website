How much does a detail cost?

Most premium details are $350 to $500, depending on the size and condition of the vehicle. Paint correction, ceramic coating and interior shampooing cost more. Estimates are always free.

How long does it take?

Premium details take 3 hours or more and maintenance details about 2 hours. Paint correction and ceramic coating take longer, and we'll tell you up front.

Do you need my water?

No. We bring our own spot-free water, which is perfect for detailing safely in the Florida sun.
