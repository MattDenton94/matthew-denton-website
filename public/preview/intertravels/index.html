Before you book

Good to know.

Can't see your question? Message Jason.

Is the tour just for my group?

Yes. Every tour is private: your group, a private vehicle, and Jason as your guide and driver. The Private Group Tour covers up to 5 guests. Larger groups can ask for a quote.

Where do you pick up from?

Your hotel, guesthouse or Airbnb anywhere in Cape Town, the Winelands and surrounds.

What's included, and what isn't?

Transport, guiding, and driving and waiting time are included. Entrance fees, wine tastings and third-party activities like the cable car, seal cruise or paragliding are paid separately on the day.

How do I secure my date?

Send a request through the booking form or WhatsApp. There's no payment to enquire. Your date is held once Jason confirms it with you by WhatsApp, email or phone, usually within one business day.

How are safari prices worked out?

The Aquila day trip starts from R4,000. Overnight safaris at Inverdoorn, Sanbona and Gondwana start from R10,000 per group, and lodge rates change with the season, so Jason sends an exact quote for your dates.

Can I see prices in my currency?

Yes. Use the currency switcher at the top of the page for an approximate price in US dollars, euros or pounds. Final prices are confirmed in South African Rand.
