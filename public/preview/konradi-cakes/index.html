The detail

Beautiful to look at. Even better to eat.

Nelly believes a wedding cake should taste as good as it looks. Every cake is baked from scratch with free-range eggs, creamy butter, fresh zests and preserves.

Iced cakes are coated in Belgian chocolate ganache and the finest couture fondant, then finished by hand with the delicate detail couples fall in love with.

  • Personal design consultation and tasting in Stoke Fleming
  • Delivered and set up at your venue on the morning of the wedding
  • One cake designer from first sketch to final placement

Meet the maker

Hi, I'm Nelly.

I've been baking for friends and family since I was young, and in 2009 I turned that passion into Konra'di Cakes. Today I design bespoke wedding and celebration cakes for couples across the South Hams.

I work from home, which means you deal with me personally, from the first consultation to setting up your cake at the venue. My designs have been published in industry magazines and recognised at the South West Wedding Awards.

"I strive for my cakes to not only look fantastic but taste delicious too."
