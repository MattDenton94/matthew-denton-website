## What it needed

Nectra needed a store built around a single hero product — a foldable MagSafe power bank — not a general catalogue. That kind of store lives or dies on the product page: the price has to feel justified, the trust signals have to show up right where someone's deciding whether to commit, and the reasons to buy now instead of shopping around have to be obvious in a few seconds of scrolling.

## What I built

A dark, product-led Shopify build where the hero leads with the discount and the benefit — "Never run out of battery again" — not the brand name. The product page carries checkout trust badges (cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Zapper) right at the point of decision, plus a comparison table that does the selling a paragraph of copy can't: three built-in cables, a fold-out stand and wireless charging, ticked off against "the others." Real customer testimonials sit further down, in their own words.

## The result

A store built to turn a stranger clicking an ad into a checkout, not to look impressive in a portfolio — which is exactly what a single-product Shopify store is supposed to do.
